## What's new
- **Instant mode.** Drop videos and Unmuffle cleans them up right away, saving each one next to the original as `name-1`. No player, no Export button, nothing to confirm. Drop as many as you like; they're done one after another.
- The Audio, Voice and Background Noise choices sit small on the right and apply to every video you drop.
- Instant is the first of the three modes at the top of the window.

> [!NOTE]
> Already on 1.3? Unmuffle updates itself: click **Update** in the banner, or choose **Unmuffle → Check for Updates…**.

<details>
<summary><b>First time opening Unmuffle?</b></summary>

Unmuffle isn't notarized by Apple, so macOS blocks it the first time. Click **Done**, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, and click **Open Anyway** next to Unmuffle. If macOS says the app is damaged, run this in Terminal:

```bash
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Unmuffle.app
```
