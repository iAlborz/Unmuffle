# Unmuffle Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Unmuffle does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- Your videos are processed entirely on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
- The app makes no network connections. It has no analytics, tracking, ads or accounts.
- The only files Unmuffle reads are the videos you open, and the only files it writes are the copies
  you choose to save.
- Your settings (Simple or Advanced mode, Auto-play) are stored only on your Mac.

If you have questions, open an issue at https://github.com/iAlborz/Unmuffle/issues.
